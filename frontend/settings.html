Study OS operates on Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS). You can configure your credentials in backend/.env:

SUPABASE_URL=https://your-project.supabase.co
SUPABASE_KEY=eyJhbGciOi...
SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY=eyJhbGciOi...

To apply the tables, execute the schema located at backend/database/schema.sql in your Supabase SQL editor.
